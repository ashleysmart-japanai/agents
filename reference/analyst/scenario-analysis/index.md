# Scenario Analysis

- **Basis:** authored method guidance; validate suitability against the task evidence.
- **Shared method:** [Analytical evidence and handoff](../methods/evidence-and-handoff.md).

## Inputs

- Decision, time horizon, baseline, external drivers, model relationships, constraints, and candidate future conditions.

## Method

- Separate scenarios describing possible futures from forecasts of likely outcomes.
- Define internally consistent combinations of drivers rather than independently selecting every best or worst value.
- Name the mechanism connecting each driver to the result.
- Apply identical accounting rules and horizons across scenarios.
- Include a severe but plausible stress case and a reverse stress case that finds failure conditions.
- Do not attach probabilities or compute weighted expected outcomes without a defensible probability model.
- Identify actions robust across scenarios and actions that depend on one future.
- Define observable signposts that would trigger a change in plan.

## Deliverable

- Scenario assumptions, comparable outcome table, vulnerabilities, robust actions, and monitoring triggers.

## Validation probe

- A capacity-limited operation should never deliver more than its capacity in an upside-demand scenario unless capacity is explicitly changed.
