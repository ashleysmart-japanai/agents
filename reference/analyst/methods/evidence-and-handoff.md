# Analytical Evidence and Handoff

- **Purpose:** support reproducible decisions from data without overstating what the evidence identifies.
- **Decision:** record the question, owner, population, unit of analysis, time horizon, and decision threshold.
- **Evidence:** record source paths or queries, extraction time, definitions, units, filters, and sample counts.
- **Assumptions:** label observed facts, estimates, elicited assumptions, and unresolved inputs separately.
- **Missing inputs:** ask for facts that change the method; use labeled illustrative inputs when a demonstration is requested.
- **Integrity:** inspect missingness, duplication, measurement changes, selection bias, and dependence before modelling.
- **Uncertainty:** distinguish outcome variability, parameter uncertainty, structural uncertainty, and numerical estimation error.
- **Reproducibility:** retain executable analysis, input snapshot or reproducible query, parameters, software versions, and random seeds when applicable.
- **Execution:** run the analysis before claiming numerical results; describe unexecuted work as a plan or illustration.
- **Validation:** compare to a simple baseline or known result and check units, bounds, edge cases, and method-specific assumptions.
- **Handoff:** provide the finding, supporting evidence, uncertainty, limitations, decision implications, and next useful evidence.
- **Privacy:** keep identifiable records out of reusable skill references and generated knowledge graphs.
- **Knowledge boundary:** retrieve method guidance from the selected analyst skill graph and its allowlisted reference pages.
- **Task data:** analyze user-authorized datasets separately from the method knowledge graph.
- **Graphify:** code-dependency analysis is a separate gated workflow; this curated graph is not a Graphify index.
- **Provenance:** these methods are newly authored analytical guidance, not claims extracted from the DS/FDE training slides.
