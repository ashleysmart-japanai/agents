# Data Visualization and Reporting

- **Basis:** authored method guidance; validate suitability against the task evidence.
- **Shared method:** [Analytical evidence and handoff](../methods/evidence-and-handoff.md).

## Inputs

- Audience, decision, data or validated analysis, metric definitions, denominators, units, time period, and uncertainty.

## Method

- Select a chart for the comparison, trend, distribution, relationship, or uncertainty being communicated.
- Preserve meaningful baselines; disclose truncated or logarithmic axes.
- Label units, population, time range, denominators, and data source.
- Show uncertainty and missing data when they affect interpretation.
- Use consistent scales and comparable aggregation across groups.
- Avoid causal titles for observational relationships.
- Use accessible labels and avoid relying on colour alone.
- Use standard plotting tools for exportable figures; provide a standalone chart and underlying summary table.
- Lead the brief with the decision-relevant finding and its limits.
- Keep observed findings separate from assumptions and proposed actions.

## Deliverable

- Annotated charts or tables, accessible captions, source and metric definitions, and a concise evidence-based decision brief.

## Validation probe

- Compare groups with different sample sizes using explicit denominators; a bar chart with a truncated axis should be corrected or clearly justified.
