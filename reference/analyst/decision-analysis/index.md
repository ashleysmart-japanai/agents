# Decision Analysis

- **Basis:** authored method guidance; validate suitability against the task evidence.
- **Shared method:** [Analytical evidence and handoff](../methods/evidence-and-handoff.md).

## Inputs

- Options, feasible actions, uncertain states, probabilities if supported, outcome table, constraints, horizon, and decision-maker preferences.

## Method

- Include the status quo and identify infeasible or dominated options.
- Use a consistent horizon and units across the payoff table.
- Distinguish expected monetary value from expected utility and explicit risk constraints.
- Use stated risk preferences rather than inventing weights or utility functions.
- Evaluate expected outcomes, downside exposure, and regret where relevant.
- Treat unsupported probabilities as scenario assumptions rather than calibrated forecasts.
- Calculate break-even probabilities or costs to show when the ranking changes.
- Bound the value of further information by expected value with perfect information minus the best current expected value.
- Subtract information costs when judging whether research is worthwhile.
- Keep a model-based recommendation distinct from the accountable person's decision.

## Deliverable

- Option table, assumptions, preferred action under stated preferences, downside and switching conditions, and whether further evidence is worth collecting.

## Validation probe

- For equal-probability states with payoffs A=(100,0) and B=(40,40), expected-value choice is A at 50 and perfect-information value is 20 before research costs.
